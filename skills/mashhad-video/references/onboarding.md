# Creative intake before production

Read for a new film or an unresolved change of direction. Ask in the user's language even though these instructions are written in English. A local revision or a resumable project keeps its established brief.

## Ask one compact round

Recover answers from the conversation and supplied materials first. Ask only what remains undecided, normally three to five grouped questions in one message. Offer a few plain-language options with a recommended direction; allow the user to delegate the choice. Fit the host's question-tool limit by combining related topics rather than sending a long series of forms.

| Topic | Example question | Useful choices or details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purpose | What are we promoting or explaining, who is it for, and what should viewers do next? | Product launch, brand awareness, explanation, sign-up or purchase CTA |
| Delivery | Where will it appear, and roughly how long should it be? | Reels/Shorts vertical, website/presentation landscape, square social post; suggest a duration if unknown |
| Visual direction | Which design direction feels right? | Clean premium typography, bold energetic graphics, cinematic depth/3D, playful illustration; welcome a reference link or existing brand example |
| Content and identity | What must appear, and what assets or copy do you already have? | Language, exact claims, logo, colors, product screenshots, URL/QR; distinguish supplied copy from copy to draft |
| Audio | Do you want effects only, human-sounding AI narration plus effects, or an existing voice recording? | For narration, establish language, accent and restrained versus energetic delivery; music remains off unless explicitly requested |

For a broad request such as "Make an advert for my app," ask this intake before choosing a final style or generating media. Do not require a novice to choose a renderer, codec, frame rate, easing curve, or API model. Resolve those implementation choices after the creative answers.

Wait for missing decisions that materially change the film. Inspect available tools and supplied assets while waiting. If the user says "you choose" or has already supplied a complete brief, state the selected assumptions and continue. Do not force five questions, request the same answer twice, or turn a one-line correction into a new briefing session.

## Turn the answers into a direction

Summarize the message, audience, CTA, delivery format, visual treatment, shot progression and audio in a compact production brief. Recommend a coherent direction tailored to those answers; the promotional example is a quality reference, not a mandatory orange-and-cream template.

For a substantial film, establish a representative styleframe and a short motion test before the full render. Follow the existing art-direction and review references. The user need not approve every intermediate artifact unless they asked for that review process. Quality is established through the rendered result and its review, not through an engine count or a promise of an instant first-pass masterpiece.

## Route the audio choice

- **Effects only:** continue with sparse, licensed or original effects and nonmusical atmosphere. Do not request an ElevenLabs account just to start the film.
- **Human-sounding AI narration:** load [elevenlabs-audio.md](elevenlabs-audio.md). Check the integration, request installation/connection only if missing, and use the Haytham preference for Arabic unless the brief specifies another voice or accent. Make clear this is generated narration, not a recording by a hired human actor.
- **Existing recording:** inspect and time that recording. Do not replace it or require the plugin unless the user asks for regeneration.

Settle the creative direction first, then request provider setup when the chosen audio route needs it. Continue independent visual work while connection is pending, but report narration as pending until a usable voice asset exists.
